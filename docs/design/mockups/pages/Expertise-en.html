What I mean by humanitarian work

All my work started here, with a displaced community in 2012. I learned that protection is not a system an organization builds and then invites people into. It is trust, built with the people the system is meant to protect.

Today I work on three kinds of projects: referral and protection systems run by residents, negotiating access when interests collide, and reviewing existing programs that do not reach the people they were designed for.

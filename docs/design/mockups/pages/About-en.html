About

I learned to ask before I plan. I still do.

I am a consultant in management, governance, humanitarian work and development. I work with ministries, organizations and companies, and with the people they serve.

In short

My first year of work was with a displaced community, and I knew almost nothing. What I learned there has stayed with me: a plan that the people who will live it cannot understand will not be carried out, however good it looks on paper.

After that I ran a protection program for an international organization, then worked with government bodies on administrative reform and governance manuals. Since 2020 I have worked independently, and I choose projects where I can stay until something changes.

I live between two languages. I think and write in Arabic first, and I work in English with international organizations.

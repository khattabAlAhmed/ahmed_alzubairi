Institutional governance

Written rules that say who decides, who is consulted and who is informed. The institution writes them with me, not me about it, so the staff understand them before the managers do.

Stages
5
Duration
8–12 weeks
Past projects
9

Fits

A board that wants written rules, a ministry redistributing authority, or a family business a new generation is joining.

What you receive

  • A governance charter in language everyone understands
  • An authority matrix: who decides, who is consulted, who is informed
  • Two training sessions for the board and the executive team

Stages

  1. 1I listenInterviews with the board, managers and staff.
  2. 2I readThe current bylaws, and what actually happens.
  3. 3We write togetherA first draft in two workshops.
  4. 4We testThree months on real decisions.
  5. 5We adoptA final version, and training.

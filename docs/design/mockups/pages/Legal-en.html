Privacy policy

I write this page in plain language because you deserve to know what happens to your data without needing a lawyer. If anything is unclear, ask me.

What I collect

  • When you write to me: your name, email, type of organization and your message.
  • When you create an account: your name, email and phone number, and whatever you choose to add about your organization.
  • When you request a consultation: what you write and the files you attach.
  • Anonymous statistics about which pages are read, only if you agree.

Why I collect it

To reply to you, to follow your consultation, and to learn which of my writing is useful. I don't sell your data, and I share it only with the services the site runs on, and only as much as they need.

Protected documents

When I give you access to a document, every opening is logged, and a watermark with your name and the viewing date appears on it. This protects the document's owners, and it protects you too.

Conversations and attachments are kept until the administrator deletes them. Only I and the people I assign to the consultation can see them.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask me to correct it, or close your account. Write to me at hello@example.org.
